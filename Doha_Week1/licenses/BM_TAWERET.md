Credit: Daniel Pett / © Trustees of the British Museum.

MuseAI derivative: converted the published mesh to GLB, reduced geometry to approximately 65,000 triangles, resized photo textures to at most 2048 px, and adjusted orientation and uniform exhibition scale. No missing archaeological features were invented. Preview images are renders of this derivative. Distributed under the same source license.

The exhibition scale is illustrative and is not a measurement of the ancient object. The original high-resolution sources remain available at the source link.
